Description: 	This model was based on the LRRP (Long Range Recon Patrols) typical during the Vietnam conflict. LRRP's 
                were used to conduct long days of exhausting patrols (5-10 days min.) off of normal trails, to conduct recon 
                and engage hostile targets if necessary. LRRP's were a cructial element of the war effort, providing much
                needed intel in addition to the removal of key enemy targets.
